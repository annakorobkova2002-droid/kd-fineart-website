About

Twenty-five years of trusted relationships

KD Fine Art Monaco provides high-end art consulting and collection management services to private and corporate clients. The firm was founded by Ekaterina Dolgova, a Monaco-based art advisor and dealer.

Ekaterina brings more than 25 years of experience in the art market, ranging from Old Masters to Modern and Contemporary art. As curator of a museum-quality collection for an ultra-high-net-worth private client, she built strong relationships with galleries, private dealers and artists.

She went on to partner with Maison d’Art Gallery, exhibiting at leading international fairs including TEFAF and Masterpiece. In 2018 she founded KD Fine Art, which sources exceptional works for its clients while making collecting an enjoyable and instructive experience.
