For more than twenty-five years, from Old Masters to Modern and Contemporary art, KD Fine Art has guided private and corporate collectors with knowledge, access and complete discretion.

The Founder

Ekaterina Dolgova

A Monaco-based art advisor and dealer, Ekaterina began her career as curator of a museum-quality private collection, building lasting relationships with galleries, private dealers and artists.

She went on to partner with Maison d’Art Gallery, exhibiting at TEFAF Maastricht and Masterpiece London, before founding KD Fine Art in 2018.
